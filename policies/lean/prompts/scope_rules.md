=== The lean brief: two more rules (where they differ from the structure above, they win) ===
- **The pitch's own words are the definition of done.** Every thing, kind and event the pitch
  names is in a core flow and in the Definition of done, in the pitch's own words, each with
  an end state that shows it on its own. When the pitch lists kinds ("changes a default,
  deprecates something, or narrows what it covers"), each kind gets its own observable end
  state ("a changed default is recorded as a shift"), never one line such as "changes are
  classified correctly". A flow the pitch's words don't need is not a core flow: move it to
  Later.
- **Mint nothing the pitch didn't ask for.** No operator settings, roles or permission
  levels, invitations, approval or review workflows, audit logs, notification channels or
  admin pages unless the pitch names them or a core flow cannot work without them. "Operator
  settings" may be empty: then write "None: the pitch asks for none." Domain defaults are
  still chosen and marked (default). The safe defaults of "Install and run" stay: they are
  not features (localhost by default, an empty fresh install, and one sign-in for the person
  who runs it when it serves a network).
